Privacy

DevToolBox tools process your input entirely in your browser. Text you paste or files you open are never uploaded to a server — there is no backend involved in any tool's core function.

We do not use cookies for tracking. A theme preference (light/dark) is stored locally in your browser via localStorage and never transmitted anywhere.

If analytics or advertising is ever added to this site, this policy will be updated to accurately describe those services before they are enabled.
